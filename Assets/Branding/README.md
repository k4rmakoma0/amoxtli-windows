# Icono de Amoxtli

Diseño original generado con la herramienta integrada **ImageGen**: un códice plegado en tonos jade, papel y ocre, con la M y la flecha descendente de Markdown. La ornamentación es una interpretación contemporánea de inspiración mesoamericana, sin pretender reproducir escritura histórica.

- `Amoxtli-master.png`: original RGBA, 1254 × 1254, fondo transparente.
- `../Amoxtli.ico`: icono de Windows con imágenes de 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 128 y 256 píxeles.
- `../Amoxtli.*.png`: recursos del manifiesto para escalas 100, 125, 150, 200 y 400, y variantes sin placa para la barra de tareas.

Los recursos anteriores de la plantilla se conservan. El proyecto y el manifiesto usan los recursos nuevos con prefijo `Amoxtli`.

Para regenerar tamaños y formatos sin cambiar el arte original:

```powershell
.\scripts\Build-IconAssets.ps1
```

Prompt utilizado, literalmente:

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a single production Windows 11 desktop app icon for a native Markdown reader named Amoxtli. Primary request: combine the recognizable Markdown symbol (a bold capital M and a simple downward arrow) with an open Mesoamerican accordion-fold codex/book inspired by Nahua and Maya codices. Make a refined Fluent-style icon, crisp and exceptionally legible at 24–48 px: a compact open cream parchment codex with three broad accordion panels, warm ochre page edges, dark jade/teal covers and strong clean dark ink shapes. Integrate the bold capital M on the main left panel and an unambiguous simple down arrow on the main right panel, as the only lettering. One restrained geometric stepped-fret motif on the cover edge evokes a codex without small intricate glyphs. Contemporary cultural inspiration, not a purported reproduction of authentic writing. Slight three-quarter perspective with very shallow sculpted depth, broad flat surfaces, softly rounded precise edges, subtle Fluent shading, restrained elegant color. Composition: one centered standalone icon occupying approximately 86% of a square canvas, even clear margins, actual transparent alpha background around the silhouette. No square tile behind it, no scene, no hands, no extra objects, no wordmark, no app name, no tiny decorative text, no watermark, no multiple options, no drop shadow outside the compact silhouette. The Markdown M and down arrow must remain the primary readable shapes.
```
